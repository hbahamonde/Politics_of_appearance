## Shared prompt structure

- Use case: photorealistic-natural.
- Asset: three-panel matched-identity portrait stimulus for a Finnish academic
  online survey experiment.
- Subject: one fictional Finnish municipal-council candidate, ordinary rather
  than model-like, with no resemblance to a real public figure.
- Panels, left to right: contemporary progressive-coded self-presentation;
  neutral everyday-professional self-presentation; conservative-coded formal
  self-presentation.
- Fixed across panels: facial identity, skin tone, apparent age, expression,
  posture, camera angle, crop, distance, background, and lighting.
- Variable across panels only: clothing, grooming, hair arrangement, subtle
  makeup where applicable, and small accessories.
- Avoid: text, logos, flags, party symbols, campaign signs, real politicians,
  celebrity resemblance, caricatures, exaggerated stereotypes, beauty
  retouching, artifacts, and extra people.
